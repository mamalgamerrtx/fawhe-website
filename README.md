# FAWHE Foundation Website

The official website for **FAWHE Foundation** (Faqeri Afghan Women's Health & Education Foundation), a California-based nonprofit that helps Afghan women and girls continue their education through online high school, accelerated diploma completion, and university study abroad.

**Live site:** https://fawhefoundation.org/

---

## About the Organization

FAWHE was founded by Afghan women who know firsthand the barriers and dangers Afghan girls face when seeking an education. The foundation's guiding belief is that education can heal, empower, and rebuild lives.

**Tagline:** Educate, Empower, and Heal.

### Programs

| Program | Length | Who it serves |
|---|---|---|
| **High School Diploma Program** | 4 years | Afghan girls beginning or continuing high school. FAWHE sponsors tuition, online learning tools, and academic support toward an internationally recognized diploma. |
| **Elder High School Completion Program** | 2 years (accelerated) | Older women and young adults whose schooling was interrupted by war, restrictions, or displacement, studying alongside family or work responsibilities. |
